# KAETRAM: VERDIGRIS — The Second Age

> *A thousand years after the first heroes, their statue wakes up and starts telling everyone the story is wrong.*

## Premise

A millennium after the first age, magic is no longer a gift. It is infrastructure. Arcane-tech hums through teal conduits that light the overgrown city of **Lanternreach**, a solarpunk sprawl of greenhouse towers and moss-softened aqueducts built on the bones of the old world. Below it, the towns and dungeons of the first game lie in buried strata, half-flooded and glowing faintly. Everyone knows the story of Ellie and Felix, and no two people tell it the same way. When the oldest monument in the city starts to speak, the player becomes the one who has to work out which version is true. The truth turns out kinder, stranger and braver than any festival play.

## Ellie & Felix

**Ellie** was a cartographer and tinkerer. She was restless, quick to laugh, and the first person to hear the "song" running through ley-stone. **Felix** was a shieldbearer and keeper of people. He was steady, patient, remembered every name, and could talk a riot down to a supper. The myth says they slew the Storm-Wyrm and seized its power. In truth they sat with it for forty days, learned its language, and wove the first conduit so its magic could be shared by anyone. That act is where the whole arcane-tech age began. They lived long, happy lives and grew old together in the town they founded.

**The living legend:** the Twin Lantern Festival, where children wear paper "Ellie compasses" and "Felix shields". Rival guilds each claim the heroes' true lineage: the Wardens of the Shield and the Cartographers' Circle. A street-puppet show that gets every detail charmingly wrong. "Felix's luck" is a common blessing.

**How the player meets them:** through **Echoes**. Ellie built *memory-lenses* into the conduits, and the network itself remembers. Relics, conduit junctions and old waystones replay fragments of the pair at work: arguing over maps, sharing bread, laughing. They are not ghosts or mentors. They are a trail of breadcrumbs left deliberately, addressed "to whoever comes next".

## Starting Town: Rootwell

Rootwell is a terraced village grown into a sunken ruin plaza. Lantern-moss lights its rope bridges, and amber-windowed homes are wedged between titanic broken arches. At its heart stands the **Colossus of the Two**: Ellie kneeling with an outstretched compass, Felix standing with a shield raised over her. The stone is gone green with verdigris and ivy, roots coil through the plinth, and dead conduit-veins run up both figures. It is older than the city, and no one remembers who carved it.

**Inciting moment (minute 8):** during the festival rehearsal, the player is hiding from a rain squall beneath Felix's shield. They brush the plinth, and every conduit in the statue ignites teal for the first time in a thousand years. Ellie's stone compass turns and points down, and an Echo plays out over the square. The whole town watches Felix say something no story has ever recorded.

## What Makes It Next-Gen

- **Testimony Board:** every myth is a claim card, such as "Felix wore a crown". Players gather Echoes and relics to confirm or overturn claims, and each world server slowly builds a *shared canon*. When a claim is overturned, NPC dialogue, festival plays and even the statue's inscriptions update server-wide.
- **The Living Grid:** the conduit network is persistent and player-repaired. Routing power changes which districts are lit, where night-creatures spawn, which shops open, and which buried strata flood or drain. It works as a city-scale logistics puzzle that the whole server shares.
- **Strata Descent:** dig down through procedurally layered ruins to reach recognisable first-age locations. They are reimagined in 3D but faithful in layout, a love letter to veterans. Deeper layers hold older, rarer Echoes.
- **Light as Ecology:** a dusk/night cycle where bioluminescent fauna react to the player's lantern radius and colour. Combat turns on light: dim to sneak, flare to stagger, attune your lamp to a creature's hue to calm it rather than kill it.
- **Duet Pacts:** two players can form a bonded pact inspired by the Two. That gives combined abilities (a shield-dash into a compass-mark detonation), shared map-ink that charts fog-of-war together, and pact Echoes that only unlock when both read them.

## Zones Beyond Rootwell

1. **The Hushfen Conduits:** a flooded arcane-tech marsh where broken pylons sing and fireflies hold memories.
2. **Gallowglass Heights:** greenhouse spires where the rival guilds battle over who owns the heroes' legacy.
3. **The Wyrm's Hearth:** the colossal, dormant source of all conduits. It is still listening and still singing.

## Risks

- **Mystery-heavy narrative is costly** in an MMO. Echoes, server-wide canon and dynamic dialogue need strong tooling and a lot of writing, and players who skip lore may find the core hook thin.
- **A big leap from the source:** isometric 3D, a persistent grid and procedural strata push well past Kaetram's browser-lite roots, which risks scope creep and could alienate fans who loved its simplicity.
