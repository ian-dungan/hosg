# AFTERLIGHT

> *Two hundred years ago, Ellie and Felix carried the last light into the dark. Tonight, their statue lit its lantern.*

## Premise

The first age ended when Ellie and Felix carried the Hearthlight, the last great flame, past the edge of the world and never returned. Two centuries of golden peace followed: harvest fairs, lantern regattas, children playing "Ellie-and-Felix" in the alleys. Now a soft dusk is creeping in from the world's rim and old roads are dimming. On Kindling Eve, the heroes' stone lantern catches real fire for the first time. The light has come home looking for a new bearer, and it chooses you, a dockhand holding the festival taper. You follow their trail outward to learn what they have been doing all this time.

## Ellie & Felix

- **Ellie, the Wayfinder.** A restless cartographer who named stars after friends and never drew a map border, only "and further." Quick, funny, fearless. She held the lantern.
- **Felix, the Hearthward.** A gentle smith-knight who cooked for every camp, told awful puns and stood in front of whatever needed standing in front of. He held the shield, and Ellie's hand.
- **The legend lives on.** Wedding vows say "lantern and shield." Sailors leave two cups out on long voyages.
- **The legacy you meet.** The dark never claimed them. They became the Hearthlight's keepers, holding back the dusk from the far side. You meet them through:
  - **Lightprints**, glowing echoes that replay small moments: a soup argument, a vow, a battle plan.
  - **Breadcrumb relics**: Ellie's half-finished atlas and Felix's dented skillet-shield.
  - **A gentle mystery**: *why are they calling now?* They are tired, and ready to pass the light on.

## Lanternmere & the Twin Lamps

A tiered harbour market of slate roofs, rope bridges and ten thousand paper lanterns, spiralling around **the Twin Lamps**: bronze Ellie raising a lantern seaward, Felix's shield braced behind her, and gulls nesting in his helmet plume.

**First 10 minutes:** you set up Kindling Eve, hauling crates and learning to click-to-move through the crowd. At dusk you climb the statue to place the ceremonial taper. The stone lantern ignites gold, god rays fan across the bay, and every lantern in town leans toward it. The statue's shadow stretches across the water and points north.

## Opening Scene

> You touch the taper to the stone wick, as tradition says, though nothing has ever happened before.
>
> *Something* happens. Light pours out like warm honey, down Ellie's carved arm and across your fingers. It doesn't burn. It feels like being recognised.
>
> **Old Marrow (lamplighter, below):** "Child... come down slow."
>
> A woman made of lantern-glow flickers beside you, grinning, ink on her cheek.
>
> **Echo of Ellie:** "Felix! It *worked!* Someone finally — oh. Hello. You're small."
>
> **Echo of Felix (distant, laughing):** "Be nice. Ask their name."
>
> **Echo of Ellie:** "Right. Sorry! Would you like to go on an adventure?"

## What Makes It Next-Gen

- **Light is terrain.** Your lantern radius is server-simulated. Dusk-zones can't be walked or mapped until someone lights them, so players permanently reshape the shared world with waystones.
- **Lantern & Shield duets.** Two players bond at a shrine and share a tether that unlocks paired abilities, stance swaps and combo finishers. Solo players bond with a Lightprint instead.
- **The Living Atlas.** The map starts blank. Players chart it by exploring and sketching, then trade their maps, and the best cartographers get credited on the world map.
- **Your own Lightprints.** Leave short async echoes (a note, a pose, an emote) at meaningful places for strangers to find.
- **Town-as-character.** Lanternmere grows from server-wide contributions: ships, lighthouses, new districts. Its festivals follow the real seasons.

## Zones Beyond Town

1. **The Emberfens.** Firefly marshes where light pools in water and Felix's lost forge still smoulders.
2. **Starwrit Highlands.** Cliff observatories where every star Ellie named is a puzzle.
3. **The Rim.** The silent, inverted dusk-edge, where two figures are waiting with a lantern.

## Risks

- **Engineering.** Gameplay-critical dynamic lighting, synced across a browser MMO, is a real performance gamble on low-end devices.
- **Endgame.** A warm hand-off ending resolves the heroes' arc, so live-ops will need a second mystery.
