# SHADY GROVE 2: WINDWARD — The Sundered Sky

> *The world broke upward. Two heroes pointed the way. Go find out what they saw.*

## Premise

Ellie and Felix won the last war of old Shady Grove by ending it at the root, at the Heartstone under the continent. The world lived, but the stone's release lifted the land itself, and the land drifted apart into thousands of islands above an endless sea of cloud. Three generations later the sky is home. Folk sail between islands in wind-ships with patchwork sails, read the currents like roads, and swap rumours of islets no chart has caught. Islands drift on slow orbits, so the map is never the same twice. Now the drift is speeding up. Old routes are tearing apart, and something far above the Cloudline has begun to *answer*.

## Ellie & Felix

**Ellie** was the pathfinder: quick, laughing, reckless in the best way, never without a charcoal stick and a half-finished map. **Felix** was the steady one: a tinkerer and shield-bearer who mended what Ellie broke, built the first kite-sail from a cloak and tent poles, and could talk a frightened town into courage. Ellie found the way and Felix made it possible.

They did not die. After the Sundering they took their little ship, the *Second Chance*, past the Cloudline to keep the drifting world from scattering for good. They never came back down, and nobody thinks of them as lost. Sky-folk say *"fair winds to the Two"* when they launch. Children play "Ellie and Felix" with sticks and bedsheets. Captains still pin their oldest charts beside the helm.

**How the player meets their legacy:**
- **Wayglass shards:** pieces of Felix's old spyglass lens, scattered across the islands. Each one replays a moment of the heroes' voyage as a warm, golden *echo* on the spot where it happened.
- **Ellie's Margins:** her original maps, crowded with jokes, doodles and riddles, and still the most accurate charts anywhere. They also hold *hidden routes* that only open when the drift lines up.
- **Pim:** the cheerful, slightly vain ship-spirit of the *Second Chance*'s figurehead. Pim fell back to the world long ago and chooses the player as its new captain. Pim is a companion, not a stand-in for the heroes, and full of affectionate stories about both of them.

## Starting Town: Shady Grove

Shady Grove is a terraced sky-port built into a coral-gold island. Its stair-streets drop past windmills, bunting and swaying dock-cranes to piers that jut straight out over the clouds. The **Plaza of the Two** sits at its heart. On its bronze-and-verdigris statue, Ellie stands mid-stride with her arm flung out, pointing at an empty patch of sky. Felix has a hand on her shoulder and holds his spyglass to his eye, sighting along her arm. For seventy years nobody has known what they were pointing at.

**Inciting moment (minute 8):** at the solstice sun-crossing, light runs through the statue's real Wayglass lens and throws a beam into the sky. Where it lands, an island **surfaces from the cloud** for the first time since the Sundering. It is the *Beacon*, and the docks erupt. The old chart-keeper realises that the drift is no longer random. *Someone up there is steering it.*

## What Makes It Next-Gen

- **Living Orrery:** every island moves on a real server-side orbit. Routes, trade prices and dungeon access shift hour by hour, and players publish *Drift Almanacs* that forecast where things will be.
- **Crewed Shipwrighting:** players build ships from modular hulls, sails and engines, and the physics responds to what they build. Party members take stations (helm, rigging, gunnery, lookout), so a crew that works well together sails faster.
- **Wind as Terrain:** visible current-ribbons, thermals and storm fronts work like roads. Gliders and grapnels let you leap off cliffs, ride an updraft and land on a moving island.
- **Cartography as Progression:** a map you draw is a tradeable, sellable item. The first player to chart a minor islet names it, and the name appears for everyone on that shard.
- **Duet Echoes:** two-player co-op replays of the Wayglass memories. One player plays Ellie's way (scout, solve, sprint), the other plays Felix's (build, shield, steady). It is literally a puzzle of trust.

## Three Zones Beyond Town

- **The Beacon Shelf:** a newly risen garden-island where the grass glows and the lighthouse is still lit, tended by no one anyone can see.
- **Kitewreck Archipelago:** a rust-and-coral graveyard of old sky-ships, now home to scavenger clans and mechanical gulls.
- **The Undertow:** the dim, glittering undersides of islands, hung with roots, crystal stalactites and gravity-flipped ruins of the old continent.

## Risks

- **Scope and netcode:** moving islands, ship physics and crewed stations are far heavier than click-to-move. A browser MMO could buckle unless the drift is tightly authored and the shards stay small.
- **Mystery debt:** "where are Ellie and Felix?" is a strong hook, but players will demand an answer. A reunion that is warm, satisfying and not tragic is hard to land without shrinking the legend.
